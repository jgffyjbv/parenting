Meet Ruchy Spitzer

Parenting, taught with warmth — and a relationship-first heart.

For over ten years, Ruchy Spitzer has taught parenting classes to mothers who want a calmer, closer home. Her approach is simple but powerful: put the relationship first, and the rest follows.

Until now, her courses ran quietly by phone — passed mother to mother. This is her work, finally online: the same warmth, the same practical tools, now open to families everywhere.

“Connection isn't a reward for good behavior — it's how good behavior grows.”
